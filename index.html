OUR STORY

From a cinema café to a legend

In September 1953, a small canteen opened inside the Paradise cinema theatre at Paradise Circle, Secunderabad. Founders Ghulam Hussain and Hussain Hemati served the Hyderabadi food the city loved — and the biryani never left the menu, or the city's heart.

1953

The café opens

A small canteen in Paradise cinema theatre, Paradise Circle, Secunderabad. By the early 1960s it is a 100-seater serving Hyderabadi cuisine.

1978

A. Hemati takes charge

The next generation takes the reins and sharpens the kitchen's focus on dum biryani.

1983

Persis & the Takeaway

Paradise Persis Family Restaurant and Paradise Takeaway open in Secunderabad. In 1987 the theatre closes — the café carries on.

1996

The modern Paradise

Major restructuring and modernisation — the legend grows across Hyderabad and beyond, one handi at a time.

WHY PARADISE

How dum works

01

Layered by hand

Fragrant basmati layered over marinated meat, saffron milk, mint and birista — never mixed, always layered.

02

Sealed & slow-cooked

The handi is sealed with dough and cooked on dum, so the steam perfumes every grain instead of escaping.

03

Opened fresh

The seal breaks only when it's time to serve — with raita and mirchi ka salan, the way it has been since 1953.
