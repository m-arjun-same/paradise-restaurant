Our Story

From a cinema café to the world's favourite biryani

Paradise began in September 1953 as a small canteen and café in the Paradise cinema theatre, Secunderabad — a 100-seater serving typical Hyderabadi cuisine, founded by Ghulam Hussain and Hussain Hemati.

1953

The café opens beside Paradise cinema in Secunderabad, serving cinema-goers Hyderabadi biryani and café fare.

1978

A. Hemati takes complete charge from his father and uncle, and renovates the restaurant for changing times.

1983

Paradise Persis Family Restaurant and the hugely popular Paradise Takeaway open in Secunderabad.

1996

A major restructuring and modernisation — Paradise expands, and the legend travels across Hyderabad and beyond.

Dum Pukht

How the dum works

1

Layered by hand

Marinated meat and long-grain basmati are layered in the handi with saffron, mint, and golden fried onion.

2

Sealed & slow-cooked

The handi is sealed and slow-cooked on dum, so the steam cooks the rice in the meat's own aroma — nothing escapes.

3

Opened at the table

Served straight from the dum with raita and mirchi ka salan, exactly as it has been since the 1960s.
