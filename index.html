Layer 01 · The Crown

Saffron
Rice

Long-grain basmati, layered by hand and stained gold with saffron. The top of the handi — the first thing the steam kisses when the seal breaks.

Layer 02 · The Crunch

Fried Onion
& Mint

Golden birista — onions fried slow until they shatter — scattered with hand-torn mint between every layer of rice. Sweet, crisp, green.

03
Layer 03 · The Heart

The
Meat

Chicken or mutton, marinated and resting at the bottom of the handi, where the heat is honest. It cooks in its own juices and perfumes every grain above it.

04
Layer 04 · The Fire

Dum Fire
& Time

Sealed handi, low fire, patience. Paradise has cooked this way since a small café beside the Paradise cinema in Secunderabad — here is the fire's own timeline:

1953

Ghulam Hussain & Hussain Hemati open a 100-seat café in the Paradise cinema theatre, Secunderabad.

1978

A. Hemati takes complete charge from his father & uncle and renovates for changing times.

1983

Paradise Persis Family Restaurant and the hugely popular Paradise Takeaway open.

1996

A major restructuring and modernisation — the legend travels across Hyderabad and beyond.
